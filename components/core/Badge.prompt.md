A small pill for status, counts or metadata (dates, "Sold out", "New", track names).

```jsx
<Badge variant="accent">Call for Papers</Badge>
<Badge variant="success" dot>Tickets live</Badge>
<Badge variant="outline">28 May 2026</Badge>
```

Variants: `neutral`, `solid`, `accent`, `purple`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`, `outline`. `dot` adds a leading status dot.
